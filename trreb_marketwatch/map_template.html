Reading these numbers. Averages are not quality-adjusted: a month with more expensive sales lifts the average without prices rising, and small areas are noisy. Medians are steadier. TRREB suppresses community figures when there were two or fewer sales, and says its quarterly community figures may not match sums of its monthly reports.

Sources. Toronto Regional Real Estate Board Market Watch and Community Market Reports, as published (TRREB retains copyright; this page is for private analysis).
