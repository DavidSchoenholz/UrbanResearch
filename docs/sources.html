How to read this

This is the study's inventory, copied as written. The "Checked" column shows whether the study actually opened and verified the source. Rows marked "Not fully verified" were not opened, or only partly (a landing page, an index listing), or rest on what the study recalled or saw in a search snippet; their details may be wrong. The Provenance line in each row's details gives the exact status. Click "Details" on a row for limitations, join keys, provenance and links.
