Snapshot of 2026-10-30

Where things stand

Early-stage work on how zoning relates to new housing in two Ontario cities, plus a list of ideas for later. None of it is a finished research finding yet. This page says what each piece is, what it produced, and how far to trust it.

The three projects

Study complete (with limits)

Toronto feasibility study

Checked whether Toronto's public data can support a study of how zoning relates to housing completions. Verdict: GO WITH LIMITS. It can, but only for some years, places and kinds of project.

This is a check on the data, not a finding about Toronto's housing.

Hamilton parcel zoning

Matched Hamilton's 177,753 property parcel polygons to the zones they sit in (all but 2 got a zone), using the City's public maps downloaded on 2026-10-30.

A map-overlay result, not a policy conclusion. The City's legal by-law text governs.

What to read first

  1. The Toronto verdict and its eight limits. The core of the work so far: what Toronto's data can and cannot support, and the study period it recommends.
  2. Hamilton zoning charts. A city-wide picture of which by-law and which zones Hamilton's parcels fall under, with the caveats that come with it.
  3. The Phase 2 pipeline diagram. How the Toronto study would be run once real data is loaded, and what is blocked until then.
  4. The data sources explorer, if you want to check where a Toronto claim comes from. Unfamiliar terms are underlined with dots; hover or tab to them, or see the glossary.

How the pieces fit

The Toronto work runs in phases. Phase 1 asked whether the data exists to study the path from zoning to completed housing, and answered "yes, with limits". Phase 2 is the code that would carry out that study; it is written and tested but has not yet been pointed at real data.

The Hamilton parcel match is a separate piece of work on a different city. It produces a table and map of zoning by parcel; it has not been used to answer a research question yet.

All three pieces live on unmerged work branches, not on main. This site copies their content as of the commits listed on the About page.
