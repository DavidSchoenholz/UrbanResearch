Phase 1 · Toronto

Can Toronto's public data show how zoning relates to new housing?

Study complete (with limits)

GO WITH LIMITS

Yes, a study is possible, but only for certain years, certain parts of the city and certain kinds of project. Eight limits shape what it can say.

What this is, and is not

A feasibility study: it checks whether the data needed to follow projects from zoning to completed homes exists, links up and covers enough years. It does not say whether zoning helps or hinders housing in Toronto. In the source's words:

Which years the data covers

Each stage of a project shows up in a different dataset, starting in a different year. A study that follows projects all the way through needs every stage at once. Shaded: the window where all four overlap below city level.
