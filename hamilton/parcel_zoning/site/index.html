    About

    Each parcel gets the zone covering most of its area. Parcels where a second zone covers at least 5% are marked split-zoned, and the popup lists every zone. Condo units sharing one footprint are drawn once.

    Hamilton is mid-way through moving to Zoning By-law 05-200; some parcels are still governed by a former municipality's by-law. Exceptions, holding provisions and appeals can change what is permitted. Check with the City before relying on it.
